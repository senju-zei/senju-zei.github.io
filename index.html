CampusFind: access control test

Pass

A lost-and-found Android app for the BulSU College of Science. I led the project and tested who can see private data.

Scope
Separate clerk and student accounts, repeated test rounds
Expected
Pickup code visible only to the approved claimant and the clerk
Actual
No leaks found across posting, claiming, approving and pickup
View full report
  1. Logged in as a student and posted a found item with hidden details.
  2. Logged in as a second student and claimed it using the hidden details.
  3. Logged in as the clerk and approved the claim, generating a pickup code.
  4. Checked that the claimant and the clerk could see the code.
  5. Logged in as other students, including ones who had posted items, and confirmed the code never appeared.
  6. Repeated the rounds several times with different items.

LAPIT: finding the real cause

Champion

IDEA Ctrl: CBMS Innovation Challenge 2026. Our team had 2 days to turn raw PSA community data into a proposal. I was the data analyst.

Scope
PSA 2024 CBMS data for all 51 barangays of Malolos
Finding
59% of households in Barangay Bangkal were offline despite having cellphone signal
Root cause
Cost, not coverage
View full report
  1. Compared 9 deprivation indicators across all 51 barangays; Bangkal ranked worst by a wide margin.
  2. Noticed the barangay had cellphone signal, so missing coverage couldn't explain 59% of homes being offline.
  3. Concluded cost was the likely barrier, tied to the 2005 relocation that moved families far from their jobs.
  4. The team proposed LAPIT: a barangay work hub with free Wi-Fi, job training and matching, and a food-aid bridge.
